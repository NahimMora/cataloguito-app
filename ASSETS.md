# Recursos visuales

## Hero de Cataloguito

Archivos finales: `public/cataloguito-hero.webp` (1536 px) y `public/cataloguito-hero-800.webp` (800 px). Generados el 27/10/2026 con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`; no provienen de Sucuchito ni de otro competidor.

Prompt final:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: responsive landing-page hero image for Cataloguito, an Argentine digital catalog SaaS. Warm editorial flat-lay showing a small independent merchant's carefully arranged products: a folded coral garment, a cream cosmetic bottle, a mustard pet accessory, a small wooden toy, a ceramic mug, and a pastry box, suggesting multiple business categories in one coherent scene. Matte warm cream tabletop, subtle coral paper shapes. Premium natural product photography, approachable and authentic rather than luxury-cold. Landscape-friendly composition that crops well to a portrait mobile card. Soft natural window light. Deep coral, cocoa brown, warm cream and butter yellow. No people, hands, logos, recognizable brands, text, watermark or UI screenshot.

Las versiones WebP se derivaron del resultado generado para reducir transferencia. El PNG intermedio no se conserva en el repositorio.

Los créditos de las fotografías de demostración preexistentes están en `public/demo/CREDITS.md`.
